Real-time Simkl scrobbling for Jellyfin

RK Simkl Scrobbler

What you watch appears in your Simkl Watching now banner as playback starts, and is marked watched when you stop. Every user on the server links their own Simkl account — and an existing Simkl history can be brought into a Jellyfin library, or sent the other way.

What it does

Scrobbling means your watch activity is reported as it happens, so Simkl always reflects where you are without you touching anything.

As you watch

Live scrobbling

Start, pause and stop are reported the moment they happen, so the title shows in your Simkl Watching now banner and your paused position follows you across Simkl-connected devices. Nothing runs on a timer.

For every profile

One Simkl account per user

Each Jellyfin profile has its own Simkl login and its own settings. A user without dashboard access can link themselves from a self-service page, so you never have to hand out administrator rights to a friend.

Both directions

History sync

Already have years of Simkl history, or a library full of watched episodes Simkl never saw? The two can be reconciled either way, with the original watch dates and play counts kept.

Beyond the basics

Rewatches and exclusions

Finishing something again is filed by Simkl as a rewatch session (Simkl Pro or VIP, since that is where Simkl offers them). Libraries you would rather keep private can be left out, per user.

When it goes wrong

A report you can paste

A Logs tab builds a diagnostic report — versions, a settings summary, the recent log lines — with no tokens in it, ready for a bug report.

Matching

Titles Simkl can't place

Items Simkl cannot match by IMDb, TMDb or TVDB id are matched by filename instead, and anything still unmatched is counted and retried after the next library scan.

The history sync, in three steps

Off by default, and set up from a single tab. Each step previews what it would change before writing anything, and the first two can be undone for seven days. Import and export also work on their own — you are not obliged to turn on the continuous sync.

  1. Simkl to Jellyfin Marks as played, in Jellyfin, what your Simkl history already lists — original watch dates and play counts included, and only for items that exist in your libraries.
  2. Jellyfin to Simkl Sends what Jellyfin has as played and Simkl does not know yet. It reads your Simkl history first, so it never overwrites a date Simkl already has.
  3. Turn on sync From then on, both sides stay aligned after each playback and each library scan, at most once an hour. A pass that would change more than 200 items waits for your confirmation.

Installing it

Two minutes, from the Jellyfin dashboard. No command line.

  1. Add the repository In Jellyfin, go to Dashboard → Plugins → Repositories → Add, give it any name, and paste this URL:
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romskidd/jellyfin-plugin-simkl-scrobbler/master/manifest.json
  2. Install the plugin Open the Catalog tab, find RK Simkl Scrobbler, install it, then restart Jellyfin.
  3. Link your Simkl account Open the plugin from the dashboard sidebar, click Log In, and enter the code shown at simkl.com/pin. The page updates on its own once Simkl accepts it. Other users of the server can link themselves from the self-service page whose link you will find on that same page.

A free Simkl account is enough. Rewatch tracking needs Simkl Pro or VIP, because that is the only place Simkl offers it.

How it differs from the official Simkl plugin

This started as a fork of the official Simkl plugin, which is still maintained and added live scrobbling of its own in version 9. It is not a replacement for it — it exists for the parts it does not cover.

Your data

The plugin talks to Simkl and to nothing else. Your Simkl token stays on your own server, in the plugin configuration, and never appears in the logs or in the diagnostic report. Requests are paced to stay within Simkl's published limits. Uninstalling the plugin, or logging out from its page, removes the link.
